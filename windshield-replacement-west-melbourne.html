What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

Windshield replacement starts with protective covers on the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond, trims the old adhesive to a thin even layer and primes any bare metal so rust cannot start under the new seal. A fresh urethane bead goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, rain sensor and moldings are reinstalled. The install takes about an hour, plus adhesive cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stabilized with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks right in front of the driver call for new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast in heat.

Sand pitting and glare

Years of highway grit make sunrise drives on US-192 and oncoming headlights hard to see through.

A leak or wind whistle

Water at the top corners or noise at speed points to a failed seal from an earlier install.

Glass broken by storm debris

Limbs and flying debris during summer storms and hurricanes crack or cave in windshields.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in West Melbourne?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer between them, and a stone chip breaks the outer layer. Every time the glass heats up or cools down it expands and shrinks, and that movement pulls at the ends of the break. West Melbourne summers make that worse: a windshield parked in the afternoon sun gets very hot, then the AC hits the inside with cold air. Potholes, slammed doors and stiff truck suspensions finish the job.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a windshield replacement in West Melbourne?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, depending on the product, the heat and the humidity. The technician gives you the exact time for your install before leaving. Driving sooner can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag depends on.

Can a windshield be replaced in the rain?

The glass and the frame must be dry for the urethane to bond, so a windshield is not installed in open rain. In West Melbourne’s summer storm season we book morning appointments or work in a garage, carport or covered parking spot.

When can I wash my car after a new windshield?

Wait at least a day before a car wash and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days. Leaving a window cracked open slightly on the first day also takes pressure off the fresh seal when doors are shut.

Does a new windshield need ADAS calibration?

If your vehicle has a camera behind the rearview mirror, the answer is usually yes. The camera has to be aimed again for the new glass so lane-keep and emergency braking work as designed. We confirm it for your vehicle when we give the estimate.
