The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in West Melbourne?

Auto glass repair in West Melbourne covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or van: sealing a rock chip with resin, replacing a cracked windshield, putting in new sunroof glass and re-aiming the driver-assist camera that looks through the windshield. Most of the damage we see starts on I-95, US-192 and Palm Bay Road, where gravel trucks and highway speed turn small stones into broken glass.

A windshield is a structural part of the car. It is bonded to the frame with urethane, it helps the roof hold up in a rollover and it gives the passenger airbag something to push against. Getting that bond right is the real job.

Signs to watch for

When Do You Need Windshield or Auto Glass Repair?

West Melbourne drivers usually call after one of these moments. If one matches what you are looking at, the glass needs attention soon.

A stone hit on I-95

A sharp crack sound, then a small star or bullseye. Call now, while a resin repair can still save the glass.

A chip grew overnight

Cold AC on a sun-baked windshield makes chips run into cracks fast, especially from May through September.

Construction traffic

New neighborhoods west of I-95 bring dump trucks and loose gravel onto Minton Road, Ellis Road and the Heritage Parkway.

A sunroof burst

Tempered roof glass can shatter from heat, a stone or a stress point, often with a loud bang while parked.

A storm dropped debris

Summer thunderstorms and hurricane season send limbs, roof shingles and lawn furniture into parked cars.

A dash warning after new glass

A lane-keep or collision light after a windshield job usually means the camera was never recalibrated.

How Windshield Replacement Works in West Melbourne

Every mobile windshield job follows the same five steps, whether the car is in a driveway in a Hollywood Boulevard neighborhood or in a business park off Wickham Road.

How Much Does Auto Glass Repair Cost in West Melbourne?

The cost of auto glass repair in West Melbourne depends on what broke and what is built into the glass. A chip repair is the simplest job. A windshield with a rain sensor, heated wiper zone, acoustic layer or heads-up display takes more specialized glass, and a forward camera adds a calibration step. Sunroof glass depends on the size of the panel and the vehicle.

Insurance makes the biggest difference. Florida Statute 627.7288 waives the deductible on windshield damage for drivers with comprehensive coverage, so many people pay nothing for a new windshield. Call for a free estimate and we will check your vehicle and your coverage before any work begins.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in West Melbourne

Will my insurance cover a new windshield in West Melbourne?

Usually, yes. Under Florida Statute 627.7288, drivers who carry comprehensive coverage do not pay a deductible for windshield damage, so most West Melbourne windshield repairs and replacements cost the driver nothing out of pocket. That rule covers the windshield only. Sunroof, side and rear glass fall under your normal deductible.

How long does auto glass repair take?

A chip repair takes about 30 minutes. A windshield replacement takes about an hour to install, and then the urethane adhesive needs one to three hours before the car is safe to drive, depending on the product, heat and humidity. Sunroof glass usually takes one to two hours.

Do you come to me, or do I bring the car in?

We come to you. West Melbourne Auto Glass is a mobile service, so the technician works in your driveway, your office lot or your company yard anywhere from Palm Bay to Viera. The address on this site is a service office, not a drop-off shop.

Can a chipped windshield be fixed instead of replaced?

Often it can. A chip smaller than a quarter, or a single crack under about six inches, can usually be sealed with resin if it is not at the edge of the glass or right in front of the driver. Describe the size and location when you call and we will tell you which fix fits.

Is it safe to drive with a cracked windshield?

A small chip outside your line of sight is not an emergency, but it weakens the glass and can spread overnight in Florida heat. The windshield helps hold the roof up in a rollover and backs up the passenger airbag, so a long crack is a safety problem. Florida law also lets officers cite drivers whose view is blocked by damaged glass.

Does a new windshield need camera calibration?

If your vehicle has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise, the camera behind the mirror usually needs to be recalibrated after new glass. A camera aimed even slightly off can react late or steer the wrong way. We check every vehicle for it before the job is booked.

Do you replace windshields on work vans and company trucks?

Yes. Fleet and commercial glass is a big part of our work in West Melbourne, where many contractors and service companies park in the industrial areas off I-95. We can schedule several vehicles in one visit at your yard, early in the day before the trucks head out.

What kind of glass do you install?

We install OEM glass from the vehicle maker or OEE glass built to the same specs, and we tell you which one before the work starts. Vehicles with heads-up displays, rain sensors, heated wiper areas or acoustic glass get glass made for those features.
