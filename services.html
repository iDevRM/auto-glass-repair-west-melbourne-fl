Mobile auto glass

West Melbourne Auto Glass handles the glass work drivers and businesses across West Melbourne, Melbourne and Palm Bay run into most, from one rock chip to a row of work vans. Every service below is mobile, and every estimate is free.

Windshield Replacement

Windshield replacement removes the whole front glass and bonds a new one to the frame. It fixes the safety problem a long crack creates: weakened glass that may not support the roof or the passenger airbag in a crash.

Drivers need it when a crack is longer than a dollar bill, reaches the edge or sits in the driver’s view. Left alone, the crack keeps growing with every pothole and every blast of AC on hot glass.

Call us today for windshield replacement in West Melbourne. Read more about windshield replacement.

Chip & Crack Repair

Chip and crack repair injects clear resin into a small break, cures it with UV light and seals the surface. It solves the most common glass problem on south Brevard highways: a fresh chip from a thrown stone.

Anyone with a chip smaller than a quarter or a short single crack should call quickly. Dirt and humidity work into an open chip within days, and heat turns many chips into cracks that can only be fixed with a new windshield.

Call us today for chip & crack repair in West Melbourne. Read more about chip & crack repair.

Sunroof Glass Replacement

Sunroof glass replacement installs a new tempered panel in a standard sunroof or a large panoramic roof after it shatters. It solves an open roof that lets in rain, heat and broken glass.

SUV and crossover owners are the most common callers, often after the glass burst while the car was parked in the sun. An open roof floods in the first afternoon storm and loose glass works into the headliner and seats.

Call us today for sunroof glass replacement in West Melbourne. Read more about sunroof glass replacement.

Fleet & Commercial Glass

Fleet and commercial auto glass covers windshields and side glass for work vans, pickups, box trucks and company cars. It solves downtime: a cracked windshield can keep a work vehicle off the road and out of compliance.

Contractors, delivery services and service companies in West Melbourne need it when a crew truck takes a rock or a van comes back from a job site with damaged glass. Ignoring it risks a citation, a failed inspection and a driver who cannot see clearly.

Call us today for fleet & commercial glass in West Melbourne. Read more about fleet & commercial glass.

ADAS Camera Calibration

ADAS calibration re-aims the forward camera mounted behind the windshield so lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise read the road correctly. It solves the hidden problem a new windshield can create: a camera that is aimed slightly off.

Owners of most vehicles built in the last several years need it after a windshield replacement. Skip it and the safety systems may warn late, nudge you toward the lane line or switch off with a dash light.

Call us today for ADAS camera calibration in West Melbourne. Read more about ADAS camera calibration.
