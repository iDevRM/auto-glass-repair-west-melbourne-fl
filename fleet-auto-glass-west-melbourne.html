What Does Fleet & Commercial Glass Involve?

Fleet work starts with a list of the vehicles and the damage on each one. We match the glass for every unit, including camera brackets and heated areas, and plan the order so the vehicles needed first are done first. Why Do Work Vehicles Need Glass So Often?

Work vans and trucks cover far more miles than a family car, much of it on I-95, US-192 and roads near active construction west of the interstate. They follow dump trucks and trailers, park on gravel job sites and run with heavy loads that flex the body. High-mileage windshields also pick up sand pitting that scatters light from oncoming headlights. Put together, a busy fleet will need glass work several times a year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you replace windshields on several vehicles in one visit?

Yes. Send us the list of vehicles and the damage on each, and we schedule them for the same visit at your West Melbourne yard or job site. The technician works through them in the order you need them back.

Do you work on box trucks and large vans?

We handle most work vans, pickups, cutaway vans and light box trucks. Some larger commercial vehicles use glass that has to be special ordered, so share the year, make and model when you call and we will confirm the part.

What are the windshield rules for commercial vehicles?

Federal Motor Carrier Safety rules limit cracks and chips in the part of the windshield the wipers sweep on commercial vehicles. Damage in that area can lead to an inspection violation. Fixing chips early keeps fleet vehicles in compliance and on the road.

Can you work before our trucks leave in the morning?

Yes. Early appointments at your yard are the most common setup for fleet customers. Remember that a new windshield needs adhesive cure time before the vehicle drives, so the earlier the start, the sooner the truck is ready.
